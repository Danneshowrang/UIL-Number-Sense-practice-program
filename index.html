Instructions

How to Use:

1. Choose a practice mode from the main menu

2. Solve problems mentally - no calculator!

3. Type your answer in the input box

4. Click "Submit Answer" to check

5. Click "Next Question" to continue

6. Use 'Skip' if you don't know the answer

7. Use 'Quit' to end any session early

8. Review missed problems to learn from mistakes


Stopwatch Controls:

• ▶ Start: Begin timing

• ⏸ Pause: Pause timing

• ↺ Reset: Reset to 00:00:00

(Stopwatch automatically starts for untimed modes)


Keyboard Shortcuts:

• Enter: Submit answer

• N: Next question (after submission)

• Esc: Skip problem

• Q: Quit practice


For Estimation Problems (*):

Answers within 5% of exact answer are scored correct


Scoring System:

• Correct answer: +5 points

• Wrong answer: -4 points

• Skipped problem: -4 points

• Blank answer: -4 points

All Problem Types

Tier 1 - Fundamental:

• Addition (3-4 digit numbers)

• Subtraction (with borrowing)

• Multiplication (2-digit × 2-digit)

• Division (even division)

• Fraction ↔ Decimal conversions

• Repeating decimal → fraction (0.4166... = 5/12)

• Percent ↔ Fraction conversions

• Squares (11² to 25²)

• Perfect cubes (2³ to 12³)

• Order of operations

• Roman numerals

• GCD of two numbers

• LCM of two numbers

Tier 2 - Patterns & Tricks:

• Multiplication by 11 trick

• Multiplication by 25 (×100÷4)

• Multiplication by 99 (×100−n)

• Multiplication by 101 (×100+n)

• Difference of squares (e.g. 47×53)

• Remainders (divisibility rules)

• Mean (average), Median, Mode

• "X is what % of Y?" and "X is what % greater than Y?"

• Percent of a number

• Unit conversions (inches/feet/yards/cups)

• Sum of series (1+2+...+n)

Tier 3 - Algebra & Sequences:

• Solve for x (linear equations)

• Binomial squares (patterns)

• Arithmetic sequences (find next term)

• Geometric sequences (find next term)

• Fibonacci-type sequences (each term = sum of previous two)

• Pythagorean triples

Tier 4 - Estimation (* problems):

• Square root approximation

• Large addition chains
